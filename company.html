M0x9_Studio


Welcome to M0x9 Studio. I'm a developer and designer, and I make stuff.

I build tools, programs, and other hardware and software things for the modern world.


How it works

Everything here is made by me, from simple scripts to full applications, and from software to hardware. That way I control the quality and how it actually works.

I build what you want, not what the market says you should want. You want a phone with 12 cameras? Sure. A watch with GPS? There it is. A program? I'll build it to spec, the way you want it, and we'll have meetings to make sure it's up to your standard.


One person, one vision

Take the delta watch as an example. It's fully made in house, by one person. And it's not for sale, because why would a 17 year old sell a half working watch?

To make things like that real, I'd need more people, real experience, and the resources to bring these ideas to life.

This whole operation, the site, everything underneath it, is made by one person. Me. Mijo0x9 is my online alias.

I wish I could do more, build bigger, and make a bigger impact. But how much can one person really do?
