About me, a passionate developer and designer


Born on 31/01/2009, I have always been fascinated by the world of technology and design. Lights, electronics, the way they work together to create something amazing. That is what I want to bring to the world.

To inspire to build, learn, experiment and, well. Fail, because that's how you learn, and knowing what went wrong is half of the battle.

I aspire to make a meaningful impact in the world of technology and design. By helping, teaching, and showing that when you master both software and hardware, you can create anything.
