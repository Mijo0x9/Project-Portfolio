Projects, more and more.


HEXNET – DeltaHex (semi-public)
A free-to-use network for device encryption,
with support for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LoRa and more.
Part of the wider DeltaHex ecosystem: a modular hub ("The Shard") with LoRa/Meshtastic,
attachable sensor modules, and time-based authentication between devices.

D.E.L.T.A – AI (private)
An AI assistant with a unique structure for speed and ease of use,
with multiple ways to communicate with the user and a sandboxed shell.
Runs on a Telegram interface with two-tier memory, a model fallback chain
and the ability to write its own tools.
Also integrated into Home Assistant as a custom conversation agent.
The D.E.L.T.A project is private due to the risk of it being used outside a sandbox, container or virtual machine.

D.E.L.T.A Watch OS
Custom firmware for an ESP32-S3 AMOLED touch watch (Waveshare).
Dual-core build with a radial app drawer, watchface engine and apps like Flashlight, Timer, 2048 and Settings.
Includes a PC Monitor applet that streams my PC screen to the watch over TCP
and sends touch back as mouse input, plus a voice recorder that uploads to my server.

Smart Home Controller
A wall-mounted controller on a 360×360 round touch display (ESP32-S3).
Five-page UI with an HSV color wheel for lights, HVAC toggle, TV + amp control with a rotary volume gesture,
all talking to Home Assistant through webhooks.

Homelab (Proxmox)
A Proxmox server running a GPU-passthrough CachyOS desktop streamed with Sunshine/Moonlight,
Home Assistant, OpenMediaVault, Jellyfin, OctoPrint for 3D printing,
and a Python container that hosts my own apps with a launcher, watchdog and backups.
